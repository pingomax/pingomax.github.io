Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. What we collect

When you connect your TikTok account, the app receives only the basic profile information and permissions you authorize (for example, display name and the ability to upload/publish videos).

2. How we use it

We use this information only to publish videos to your own TikTok account at your request. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

3. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely and used only to perform the actions you request. We do not store your TikTok password.

4. Data retention and deletion

You can revoke the app's access at any time in your TikTok settings. To request deletion of any data we hold, contact us at the email below.

5. Children

The app is not intended for users under 13.
